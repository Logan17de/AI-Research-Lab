**Last updated:** 2026-07-16

Every result is assigned a status:

- **VALID** — generated by the repaired causal pipeline or an unaffected experiment;
- **SUPERSEDED** — mechanically valid for its narrow question but replaced by a fairer protocol;
- **INVALIDATED** — cannot support the original scientific claim;
- **PROPOSED** — not yet tested.
